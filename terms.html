1. What this is

The Peach Barn ("the app") is a strength-training log and programming tool provided by Thrive by Blake ("we", "us"). It records the exercises, loads and reps you enter and suggests loads for future sessions based on that history and the program you choose.

2. Not medical advice

Nothing in the app is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The suggested loads are arithmetic on your own entries, not a judgment about your body. Consult a physician before beginning this or any exercise program, particularly if you are pregnant or postpartum, have a heart condition, high blood pressure, a joint or back injury, or any other condition affected by exercise. Stop immediately and seek medical attention if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness or shortness of breath.
